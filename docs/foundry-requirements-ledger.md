**Ledger version:** 1.0

**Scope:** OverKill-Hill-FoundRy application and its capability source/package workflow. This is a requirements ledger, not a new product PRD.

## Authority and evidence rules

`AGENTS.md` sets repository identity, universal-governance boundaries, child-repository responsibilities, and publication rules. For the application, the dated `docs/foundry-implementation-plan.md` records the September 7 scope and acceptance boundary. The owner-directed `docs/portable-skill-foundry.md` (September 27) is the current capability and portable-skill contract. Read these together: the portable-skill direction extends the workbench; it does not replace the authority chain or authorize unrelated product work.

There is no separately supplied, current FoundRy workbench PRD. The October 4 `docs/reviews/2026-10-04-equilibrium-review.json` is a dated analytical review and task backlog, not a substitute PRD or proof that backlog work is complete. Its FND-01 entry identifies the missing consolidated ledger. The dated September 7 `docs/capability-workbench-maturation.md` and `docs/foundry-implementation-plan.md` preserve historical status; their test and delivery results describe the revisions and environments recorded there, not automatically the checkout, a current deployment, owner acceptance, or a public capability release.

**Evidence labels:** `Historical complete` means the named plan/review records completion at its stated revision and environment. `Documented behavior` means repository documentation describes an implemented behavior; it does not independently prove a live run. `Open` means the stated closure evidence is not recorded in the cited authority. `Conditional gate` means it becomes mandatory when its named conversion, host, or release path is selected. `Deferred proposal` means it is not part of the required baseline until the owner adopts it. In every case, authored completeness, generated files, a checked box, source portability, and repository visibility do not by themselves prove tested behavior or publication.
